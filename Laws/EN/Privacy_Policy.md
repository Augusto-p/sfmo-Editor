**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

At **SFMO Editor**, we deeply value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy transparently explains how we handle information and confirms our commitment to protecting your data when you use our application.

---

## 1. 100% Client-Side Processing & File Location

**SFMO Editor** operates locally within your web browser (*client-side*). How your files are handled depends on where they are stored:

* **Local Files:** If you open or edit a file stored on your device, it remains strictly on your local machine. File processing occurs inside your browser's memory, and the file is never uploaded or transmitted to any external server.
* **Google Drive Files:** If you use the integration to open a file stored in Google Drive, the file remains hosted in your Google Drive account. Reading and saving data syncs directly between your browser and Google Drive; at no point is your file copied, stored, or routed through SFMO Editor servers.
* **No Dedicated Servers:** We do not own, rent, or operate servers to host, process, sync, or back up your files or personal data.
* **No Central Database:** We do not maintain user logs, usage history, or backup copies of your documents.

---

## 2. Personal Data Collection

Because all application logic runs locally within your browser:

* **We do not collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) on our systems.
* **We do not track your activity** or build user profiles.

---

## 3. Local Browser Storage

To ensure proper functionality and save your preferences (such as interface configurations or temporary session data), **SFMO Editor** may use local web browser storage technologies, including:

* `LocalStorage` / `SessionStorage`
* `IndexedDB`

All of this data resides **exclusively on your device**, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's cache and site data.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services and Integrations

While **SFMO Editor** does not store data on dedicated servers, it relies on the following third-party services for hosting and optional integration:

* **Google API Limited Use Disclosure:**
  SFMO Editor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

* **GitHub Pages Hosting:** The web interface is served via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you access the site, GitHub may automatically collect standard HTTP request logs (such as your IP address, browser type, and access timestamp) for security, infrastructure, and diagnostic purposes. For more details, please review the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
* **Google Drive Integration (Optional):** If you choose to open or save files directly from your Google Drive account:
* Authentication and file transfers occur via official Google APIs directly between your browser and Google's servers.
* **SFMO Editor** only requests the minimum permissions required to read and write the specific files you explicitly select.
* We do not read, store, or transmit your credentials, access tokens, or file contents to any external servers.
* Use of this service is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).



---

## 5. Information Security

Because your files are not transmitted to or processed on external servers owned by SFMO Editor, the risk of cloud database leaks is eliminated. The security of your data depends directly on protecting your own local device and any linked third-party accounts (such as your Google account).
