### 🔒 Privacy & Storage

**Where are my files stored when I edit them in SFMO Editor?**

Your files remain where they originated:

* **Local files:** Stay exclusively on your device's hard drive or local memory.
* **Google Drive files:** Are read from and saved directly back to your personal Google Drive account.
Under no circumstances are your files or their contents uploaded to SFMO Editor servers.

**Does SFMO Editor upload my data to any dedicated servers?**

No. SFMO Editor is a 100% client-side web application running directly in your browser. We do not own, operate, or use any servers to process, store, or analyze your files or personal information.

---

### ☁️ Google Drive Integration

**What access or permissions does the app request from my Google account?**

SFMO Editor only requests the minimum necessary permissions required to allow you to pick, open, and save the specific files you explicitly select. We never access the rest of your Drive data, nor do we store your credentials or access tokens.

**Can I use SFMO Editor without connecting Google Drive?**

Yes! The Google Drive integration is entirely optional. You can use the editor by loading and saving files directly to and from your local machine without logging into Google.

---

### 🌐 Hosting & Security

**Why is the application hosted on GitHub Pages?**

GitHub Pages allows us to serve the web interface's front-end code efficiently, securely, and transparently. When you access the site, GitHub's infrastructure processes standard HTTP requests (such as your IP address), but the content you edit is never sent to or stored on their servers.

**What happens to my data if I lose my internet connection?**

If you are working with local files, all processing happens entirely within your browser's memory, so you can continue editing offline. You will only need an internet connection to open or sync files via Google Drive.

---

### 🛠️ Support & Security

**What should I do if I find a bug or issue?**

You can report bugs, technical glitches, or request new features by opening an *Issue* on our official GitHub repository.

**Is SFMO Editor safe to use for sensitive or confidential documents?**

Yes. Because your files are never transmitted to third-party cloud servers owned by SFMO Editor, the risk of data leaks in the cloud is eliminated. Security depends entirely on the protection of your own local device and your Google account.
